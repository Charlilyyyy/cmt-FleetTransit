# Market Research

Research artifacts for CMT Fleet Transit: understanding the competitive landscape, defining differentiation, and informing MVP feature priority before requirements are locked.

## Purpose

Discovery validated the problem and recorded a **go** decision. This folder answers:

1. **Who else serves this market** — direct and adjacent competitors
2. **Where they fall short** — gaps CMT Fleet Transit can own
3. **What we must match** — table-stakes features for v1 credibility
4. **How we stand apart** — technical and product differentiators
5. **What rules apply** — privacy, child safety, and photo-proof compliance
6. **How we might charge** — B2B SaaS pricing sketches
7. **How we position** — statement and feature priority informed by gaps

## Reading Order

Start with market landscape for context, then the competitor matrix. Differentiators and compliance shape what is defensible. Pricing and positioning close the research with actionable outputs for product requirements.
